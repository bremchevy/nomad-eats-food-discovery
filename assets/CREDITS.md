## Local film

The locally hosted, silent 18-second `craving-reel.mp4` is an edited montage
of the following Mixkit clips, downloaded November 20, 2026. Each source
page identifies the clip as **Mixkit Stock Video Free License** (commercial
and personal use), not the Restricted License.

- [Meat roasting on a grill, clip 2993](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/meat-roasting-on-a-grill-2993/) — 3–9 seconds.
- [Cooking a pepperoni pizza, clip 1677](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/cooking-a-pepperoni-pizza-1677/) — 2–8 seconds.
- [Chef in black suit cooking with fire, clip 4118](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/chef-in-black-suit-cooking-with-fire-4118/) — 36–42 seconds.

[Mixkit license](https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree).

Edits: six seconds per scene, 1280×720 H.264 at 24 fps, no audio, warm
colour treatment, brief fades between scenes, web-optimized MP4. The poster
is a frame from the edited grill scene. Footage is illustrative, not footage
of the listed restaurants or their featured dishes.
